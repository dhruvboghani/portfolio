How it works

Ingest

02

Transform

Flink processes events in flight and quarantines bad ones in a dead-letter queue.

03

Store

Iceberg tables on MinIO, versioned by Nessie, commit a snapshot as data lands.

04

Query

Trino reads the lake with SQL, including any past snapshot.

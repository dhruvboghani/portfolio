Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how I work and what I build.

What do you build?

I build AI-powered software: LLM and RAG applications, voice AI agents, 3D avatar assistants, real-time data pipelines and full-stack enterprise platforms.

Which technologies do you work with?

AI and LLM work with OpenAI, Azure OpenAI and Llama; Django, FastAPI, Node.js and Laravel on the backend; Angular, React and Vue on the frontend; Redpanda, Flink, Spark, Iceberg and Trino for data; and Docker with GitHub Actions for delivery.

Can you build a voice AI agent for sales calls?

Yes. I have built calling bots with Twilio, Pipecat and an LLM that hold natural conversations, detect sentiment, score calls and update the CRM automatically.

What is a 3D avatar assistant?

It is a talking 3D character that customers or admins speak to. A customer-facing avatar answers product questions, captures leads and books appointments. An admin avatar fetches invoices, purchase orders, inventory, quotations and sales data.

Can you build a real-time data platform?

Yes. I design streaming pipelines and lakehouses with Redpanda, Flink, Iceberg and Trino, including versioned tables and time-travel queries.

Do you build multi-tenant SaaS products?

Yes. I have delivered multi-tenant platforms with role-based access, microservices, API gateways and observability.

How do I start a project with you?

Send an email or WhatsApp message through the Contact page with a short description of what you need. We can then discuss scope, timeline and next steps.

Where are you based?

I am based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

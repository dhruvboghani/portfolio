12 / Live product / JyotishGrahavani: Vedic Astrology

What it solves

A free Vedic astrology website that calculates an accurate Janma Kundali from birth date, time and place, in six Indian languages, with an Android app from the same code.

Astronomical mathSwiss Ephemeris checkedLahiri ayanamsa6 languagesAndroid appRow-level security

Visit live site ↗

How it works

  • Planetary positions are computed from orbital mathematics and checked against the Swiss Ephemeris, within about one arc-minute for the Moon and Lagna across 1900 to 2100.
  • Sidereal positions use the Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa. Birth times convert to UTC using the birthplace's time zone history, including historical daylight saving and India's 1942–45 war time.
  • Kundali with Lagna, Navamsa and Moon charts in North or South Indian style, 100 years of Vimshottari dasha, yogas and doshas with their rules shown, Ashtakavarga, panchang at birth and daily Gochar.
  • Ashtakoot Guna Milan (out of 36) with a Manglik check, daily Panchang with Rahu Kaal and Choghadiya, Hindu calendar, Rashifal, numerology and nakshatra-based baby names.
  • English, Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada, with saved charts and a family dashboard that only their owner can read.
13 / Live product / SEORankdesk: SEO Toolkit

What it solves

Every everyday SEO check and generator on one free site, so SEO teams stop jumping between a dozen separate tools.

SEO auditGoogle PageSpeed APICore Web VitalsJSON-LDNo signup

Visit live site ↗

How it works

  • Paste a URL and a Googlebot-style fetch runs 24 checks: 16 on the page (title, description, headings, alt text, canonical, Open Graph, schema, links) and 8 for server and crawling (status, HTTPS redirects, response time, compression, noindex, robots.txt, sitemap).
  • Google PageSpeed adds performance and SEO scores and Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS) for mobile and desktop.
  • Generators come pre-filled from the audited site: meta tags with a live Google preview, JSON-LD schema, robots.txt and sitemap, keyword ideas, and hashtags and bios for Instagram and YouTube.
  • Privacy first: audited sites are not stored and results go straight back to the browser. Login-protected pages can be audited by pasting their HTML.
  • Free with no page limits, supported by ads, with guides on technical, on-page and local SEO.

03 / EdTech AIMyTeacher: AI Curriculum Engine

What it solves

An AI system that generates structured curriculum content for schools at scale.

DjangoOpenAIVector DBMulti-agent LLM

How it works

  • Multi-stage pipeline: strand and sub-strand analysis, skill extraction, then AI-generated questions mapped to each skill.
  • Dual-model verification: the generating model self-reviews against strict pedagogical rules, then an independent second model validates quality.
  • Semantic deduplication with vector similarity search blocks near-duplicate questions.
  • A question joins the curriculum only after generation, both verifications and deduplication pass.
  • Audit logs at every stage for traceability and prompt improvement.

Case studies

What each platform solves and how it works under the hood.

01 / GenAI / RAGInvoice Compare AI

What it solves

Finance teams lose time matching vendor invoices against purchase orders by hand, and tax or HSN mistakes slip through. This platform automates the comparison and flags anything that does not line up.

DjangoOpenAIVector DBReact

How it works

  • An LLM extracts structured fields from both documents.
  • HSN codes are detected and compared line by line.
  • Tax variance checks catch wrong GST rates and over-billing.
  • Vector search finds similar past invoices to surface anomalies.
02 / Voice automationAI Calling Bot & CRM

What it solves

Sales follow-up calls are repetitive and the CRM is rarely updated on time. This bot holds natural conversations and keeps the pipeline current by itself.

DjangoTwilioPipecatAzure LLM

How it works

  • Twilio carries the call; Pipecat orchestrates the real-time voice pipeline.
  • An LLM (Azure) drives the conversation with memory of what was said.
  • Sentiment detection tracks how the customer feels as the call goes.
  • Status, objection, offer and follow-up are written to the CRM automatically.
03 / Data architectureReal-Time Data Lakehouse

What it solves

Event volume of 10M+ a day needs ingestion that does not fall over, and tables that can be audited and queried while data keeps arriving.

RedpandaFlinkIcebergTrinoMinIONessie

How it works

  • Redpanda ingests events with Kafka-compatible streaming.
  • Flink transforms them in flight and quarantines bad events.
  • Iceberg tables on MinIO, versioned by Nessie, give every commit a snapshot.
  • Trino runs interactive SQL on fresh data, including time-travel reads.
